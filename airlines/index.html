Reducing costs 🗒

The DRCT's goal is to make airline distribution more efficient — simple, personalised, and low-cost. We focus on technology so that airlines can focus on the product.

On reaching new markets and customers

Business development in non-domestic markets is always a challenge for airlines. Especially in markets where the market share of the airline is low. Payment collection and contracting are expensive.

DRCT is integrated with GDSs, payment getaways, and represented by global GSAs and all main markets. Using existing connectivities we make it possible to sell tickets via GDS on any market and accept payments via BSP or credit card with no hustle or GDS fees.

On supporting of own OBT and API

Developing and supporting your own OBT is expensive and isn't effective. It's almost impossible to force sellers to use OBTs of different airlines for flight searches. Delivering and supporting your own API or OBT is also a challenge — implementation slots, certification, support, and contracting. All this means that the airline spends resources on technical staff instead of developing its business.

DRCT delivers content to the seller via API or OBT. Integrated with hundreds of sellers our team clearly understands the requirements of any type of seller — TMCs, OTAs, Traditional Agencies. DRCT already has integration with mid- and back-office systems, so implementation of DRCT solutions is the easiest way to get content from all needed airlines without the need to implement each airline's OBT or API.

Increase revenues 📈

The airline business is low-margin. Using modern technology and access to markets, DRCT provides opportunities for revenue and margin growth.

By expanding the network

The traditional airline business model of building its own network and distribution via GDS — is no longer in demand. The point-to-point model with direct distribution channels is taking over. But it also has shortcomings. The absence of transit passengers and limited distribution makes this model barely scalable.

DRCT is an aggregator for many airlines, we are also connected to most CRSs and all GDSs. It makes it possible to establish partnerships quickly and easily with no changes required to business models and pricing structures. We also effectively deliver this content to sellers all over the world.

Using deep data analysis and personalisation to sell more

While distributing, airlines can get a piece of very limited information about flight requests. In most cases, it's just an anonymous pricing request and booking. Lack of information about who is asking, and actual competitor's offers make the creation of data-driven offers impossible.

DRCT receives loads of information directly from the seller at the moment of request. In most cases, we know who is searching for the flight, previous buying history, and offers from all airlines that are proposed to the passenger. It gives us a unique advantage to create personalised offers that will perfectly fit passenger expectations.
