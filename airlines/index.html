FAQ

Have a question about becoming a DRCT partner? Reach out to our team.

  • How does airline integration with DRCT work?

    Provide your NDC API credentials to DRCT. Our team handles certification, testing, and configuration, then distributes your content to all connected agencies. Integration typically takes 2–6 weeks depending on your NDC setup.

  • Which NDC standard does DRCT support?

    DRCT supports IATA NDC standard versions 17.2 and 21.3. We work with airlines across all major GDS platforms and can accommodate airline-specific extensions to the standard.

  • Do travel agencies need new credentials or training?

    No. Agencies access your NDC fares through the DRCT extension within their existing GDS. There is no new interface, no separate login, and no retraining required for agency teams.

  • How are NDC bookings settled?

    Bookings made through DRCT settle via standard BSP — the same process as your existing GDS sales. Your commercial agreements with agencies remain unchanged.

  • Can we control which agencies access our content?

    Yes. DRCT provides airline-side controls for managing content access. You can set rules by agency, by GDS, or by booking type, and monitor distribution in real time through DRCT reporting.

  • What post-sales operations does DRCT support?

    DRCT supports date changes, fare exchanges, refunds, and voids — all processed through the same workflow agencies use for standard GDS bookings. We handle the complexity so agencies don't need to contact your support team directly.

  • Is DRCT IATA certified?

    Yes. DRCT is certified by IATA as an Airline Retailing Maturity (ARM) System Provider, confirming we meet IATA's standards for NDC distribution and airline retailing.

  • How long does onboarding take?

    Most airline integrations go live within 2–6 weeks. Our team manages the certification, testing, and configuration process — you provide NDC access and we handle the rest.
