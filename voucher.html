How it works

The entire process takes under two minutes.

Step 1

Select your airline

Choose which airline voucher to activate. Each offer gives you 30 EUR off a future booking on that airline's website.

You can only activate one voucher per eligible card.

Step 2

Verify your Mastercard

Complete the secure Mastercard widget. Your card eligibility is confirmed or declined within seconds.

If your card is not eligible, no voucher is issued and no charge is made. You can retry with another Mastercard.

Step 3

Receive your code by email

Once verified, your 30 EUR voucher code is generated and sent to your registered email address.

Not in your inbox? Check spam. If it hasn't arrived within 10 minutes, contact support.

Voucher codes are generated only after successful Mastercard verification.  ·   Eligibility is checked securely — your card details are not stored.  ·   Voucher is valid according to the selected airline's terms and conditions.
