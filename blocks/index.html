Challenges in Airline Distribution — and How DRCT Solves Them

Airlines lose control of their offers across GDS and indirect channels. DRCT provides a unified layer to distribute, manage, and preserve offers consistently.

Problem

Fragmented distribution channels

Managing separate connections for GDS, NDC, and direct channels creates operational overhead. Mixing content without a unified layer leads to reconciliation errors and inconsistent workflows.

Solution

One layer. All channels.

DRCT provides a unified distribution layer where all airline offers are normalized and delivered through a single integration. Order management, reporting, and settlement handled in one place.

Problem

Limited merchandising control

GDS channels were not built for modern airline retailing. Bundles, ancillaries, and dynamic offers are either not supported or not displayed correctly, leading to lost revenue.

Solution

Control without compromise.

DRCT preserves the full structure of your offer — including fares, ancillaries, bundles, and pricing logic — and delivers it consistently across all channels.

Problem

High distribution costs

Traditional GDS models introduce multiple intermediaries, increasing cost-per-booking without proportional value. Airlines have limited control over how distribution costs scale.

Solution

Better economics at scale.

DRCT enables direct airline-to-seller distribution and supports gradual volume shift from GDS to NDC channels, improving unit economics while maintaining distribution reach.

Problem

Agent resistance to NDC

Agents rely on familiar GDS workflows. NDC adoption is slowed by operational challenges — inconsistent booking flows, NDC ADM risk, and settlement complexity.

Solution

NDC that works for agents.

DRCT delivers NDC content inside familiar agent workflows — including GDS-adjacent environments — eliminating the need to learn new systems and accelerating natural adoption.

Problem

Inefficient revenue optimization

When distribution is fragmented, airlines lose control over pricing signals. Offers are reshaped or degraded across channels, making effective revenue management difficult.

Solution

Complete pricing control.

DRCT maintains control over how offers are distributed and consumed — enabling consistent pricing, better personalization, and more accurate demand signals across all channels.

Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do IATA agents access NDC content through DRCT?

    IATA agents access NDC content through DRCT Extension (a GDS-embedded tool), DRCT Search (a standalone booking interface), or via direct API integration. All options deliver the same normalized NDC content and allow agents to book, issue, and service orders through familiar workflows.

  • What does NDC integration for agencies actually involve?

    For most agencies, integration means enabling DRCT Extension inside your existing GDS — no new systems, no workflow changes. For tech-enabled agencies, DRCT provides a full API with documentation, sandbox access, and implementation support. Either way, your team gets NDC access without managing airline-by-airline integrations.

  • How do I manage NDC orders — changes, cancellations, and refunds?

    All NDC order management is handled through DRCT's servicing layer. Agents can initiate changes, void tickets, and request refunds directly — with DRCT normalizing the servicing flow across all connected airlines. No need to contact the airline directly for routine post-booking actions.

  • Why do NDC bookings sometimes fail, and how does DRCT handle this?

    NDC booking failures can result from airline system timeouts, fare expiry, or validation errors. DRCT monitors these in real time and surfaces clear error messages with recovery guidance. For booking-critical failures, DRCT's 24/7 support team is available to assist directly.

  • Does DRCT handle the full NDC accounting workflow?

    Yes. DRCT supports BSP settlement for NDC tickets and provides reporting tools that integrate with back-office and mid-office systems. Agents can track NDC bookings alongside GDS content in a single reporting view, with structured data for reconciliation and accounting.
