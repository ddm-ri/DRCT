About DRCT

Airline distribution today is split between GDS and a fragmented set of direct channels — and these systems don't work together cleanly.

DRCT sits between airlines and travel sellers, bringing direct airline content — delivered via NDC and other custom distribution schemes, including low-cost and charter carriers — into one consistent, usable flow. Airlines distribute once. Travel sellers access everything through a single integration — alongside the GDS connections they already use.

We unify airline distribution

Direct airline content is fragmented across multiple distribution schemes — NDC, custom airline APIs, low-cost, and charters — each with different formats, booking logic, and servicing workflows.

DRCT aggregates these sources and normalizes them into a single schema. Travel sellers access full airline inventory without managing multiple integrations — and continue using their existing GDS alongside. Airlines reach every type of seller through one distribution layer.
