Why DRCT

The case for a unified airline distribution platform — in plain terms.

One platform for GDS and NDC together

Hybrid distribution without managing two parallel systems. NDC and GDS content coexist, consistently delivered to every seller.

No system switching

Airlines manage distribution in one place. Sellers book, change, and service in one place. The full workflow lives inside DRCT platform.

Beyond a traditional airline aggregator

DRCT enhances offers — adding ancillaries, building bundles, and assembling multi-airline itineraries. Content arrives at the seller better than it left the airline.

NDC access without the friction

Sellers get NDC fares and airline content through familiar interfaces. No new accreditation, no separate NDC onboarding per airline.

A transition layer, not a disruption

Airlines moving from GDS toward NDC use DRCT to manage the shift gradually — maintaining full coverage while growing direct channel volume.

Every seller type covered

IATA agencies, non-IATA agents, OTAs, TMCs, and Super Apps — all reached through the same distribution platform.

24/7 operational support

Distribution issues, booking failures, and escalations handled by DRCT's own team — for airlines and sellers alike.
