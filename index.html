FAQ

If you didn't find an answer to your question, please ask our support.

  • Which airlines already work with DRCT?

    Aegean Airlines, airBaltic, American Airlines, British Airways, Brussels Airlines, Emirates, flydubai, Iberia, LOT, Lufthansa, SWISS, TAP, United Airlines and more than 30 other airlines. Request a DEMO to get an updated list of integrations.

  • Is DRCT a ticketing agency?

    No, DRCT is a technology provider and airline aggregator. We do not sell air tickets but provide agencies and other ticket sellers with the tech solution for ticketing.

  • Will I lose my incentive agreement with the airline?

    No, all sales via DRCT will be done under your own airline agreements. If it includes incentives, you keep it.

  • What is NDC?

    New Distribution Capabilities (NDC) is an XML-based data transmission standard. NDC enables data transmission directly from air carriers to agents bypassing outdated intermediates such as GDS. It provides access to complete airline content including exclusive fares, ancillaries, and personalised offers.

  • How to get credentials from an airline?

    DRCT's team assists with the application process for airline NDC credentials. Contact us and we will help you get started with all the major airlines in our network.

  • How to start working?

    Installation takes only 10 minutes. Request a demo and our team will guide you through the setup process and 30-day free trial.

  • Which IATA office is used for ticketing?

    Ticketing is done via your own IATA office number. DRCT does not issue tickets — your agency does, using your own credentials and BSP settlement.

  • Can I trust DRCT?

    DRCT is certified by IATA as an Airline Retailing Maturity (ARM) System Provider. We work with over 40 airlines and hundreds of agencies globally.

  • How to make a refund or exchange?

    Refunds and exchanges can be processed directly within the DRCT interface or through your GDS workflow, depending on the airline's NDC capabilities.

  • What should I do if my airline's NDC lacks functionality?

    Our support team provides 24/7 assistance and can escalate directly to the airline's NDC team. We also proactively monitor NDC quality and flag issues before they affect your workflow.

  • Is it possible to get an ADM using DRCT?

    DRCT specifically protects against ADMs. Our workflows are designed to comply with airline requirements and avoid debit memos. We own the migration risk so you don't have to.

  • How is the search for cheaper booking classes working?

    DRCT automatically checks every booking for lower NDC fare classes post-purchase. If a better price is found before ticketing, you receive a notification with the option to reprice.

  • Are the queues available in DRCT?

    Yes, DRCT integrates with your GDS queues. You can monitor NDC bookings alongside your regular GDS queue workflow without switching interfaces.
