Frequently Asked Questions

What is Altitude Upgrade?

Altitude Upgrade is a digital service that helps travellers access Business Class upgrade offers for eligible flights. Depending on the airline and availability, upgrades may be offered at a special price or with an eligible Mastercard premium card benefit.

How do I check for an upgrade?

Enter your airline booking reference and last name. Altitude will check your itinerary and display available upgrade options for each eligible flight segment.

How quickly will I receive an offer?

For selected airlines, upgrade availability may be displayed within few seconds. For other airlines, our team will review your booking and provide available options within approximately 30 minutes.

Which airlines are supported?

Instant availability checks are planned for selected airlines, including Turkish Airlines, Qatar Airways, LOT Polish Airlines, and Aegean Airlines. Requests for other airlines may be processed manually. Availability depends on the specific flight, route, fare type, and airline conditions.

Is a Business Class upgrade guaranteed?

No. All upgrades are subject to airline availability, booking eligibility, fare conditions, and confirmation at the time of purchase.

What happens if my booking includes several flights?

Altitude will check each flight segment separately. You may receive different upgrade options for each eligible part of your journey.

Can Altitude monitor the upgrade price for me?

Yes. Select Monitor the Price, and Altitude will regularly check for new or improved upgrade options. You will be notified by email and through your profile when a suitable offer becomes available.

Do I need to create an Altitude profile?

A profile is required to proceed with an upgrade or activate price monitoring. Your profile allows you to: Track the status of your requests; Review upgrade offers; Manage available benefits; View your request history; Access your upgraded tickets.

How do I unlock a Mastercard premium card benefit?

You may be invited to securely validate your eligible Mastercard premium card. Once eligibility is confirmed, available upgrade discounts will appear in your Altitude profile and can be applied before payment.

Will my card be charged during validation?

Card validation is used to confirm eligibility for the benefit. Any applicable validation conditions will be clearly shown before you continue. The upgrade payment is completed separately after you review and confirm the final offer.

How do I pay for an upgrade?

After selecting an offer, you will see the full order summary, available benefits, and final price. You will then be redirected to a secure payment page to complete the transaction.

When will I receive my upgraded ticket?

Following successful payment, your upgraded ticket will normally be issued within 10–15 minutes. It will be sent to your email and added to your Altitude profile.

What happens if my payment is unsuccessful?

You will receive a notification and will be able to return to your profile to review the request or try the payment again, subject to the continued availability of the offer.

Can an upgrade offer change?

Yes. Airline availability and prices may change until the upgrade is confirmed and successfully paid. An offer is not guaranteed until the transaction is completed.

Can I cancel or change an upgrade?

Cancellation, change, and refund conditions depend on the airline and the specific upgrade offer. The applicable terms will be shown before payment.
