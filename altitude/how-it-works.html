Your Upgrade Journey

Upgrade to Business Class in a few simple steps.

  1. Fill In the Request Form

    Enter your booking reference and last name. For selected airlines, upgrade options are available in as little as few seconds. For other airlines, we send your options within 30 minutes.

  2. Choose Your Option

    Select what works best for you: 1/ Upgrade now, 2/ Monitor the price, 3/ Decline the offer. When you choose price monitoring, Altitude checks available options every hour and notifies you when a better offer becomes available.

  3. Create Your Profile

    Confirm your email and create a secure Altitude account. Use your profile to track requests, review available offers, manage benefits, and access your upgraded tickets.

  4. Unlock Your Benefits

    Validate your eligible Mastercard premium card to unlock available upgrade discounts. Review the final price, apply your benefit, and complete the payment securely.

  5. Receive Your Upgraded Ticket

    After successful payment, your upgraded ticket will normally be issued within 10-15 minutes. You will receive it by email, and it will also be available in your Altitude profile.
