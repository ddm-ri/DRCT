About Pilot Project

Your participation in the Altitude by Mastercard pilot will help us:

  • Evaluate upgrade opportunities in partnership with selected airlines
  • Understand your expectations and preferences
  • Gather feedback on your overall journey, from booking to boarding
  • Identify ways to enhance the upgrade process for a seamless premium experience

By taking part, you're helping us shape a new level of premium travel, tailored around your needs.

Why Choose Altitude

Altitude by Mastercard offers premium cardholders seamless upgrades to Business Class on selected flights, prioritizing comfort and space, and elevating the travel experience to new heights.

FAQ

    Getting Started
  • How do I request a Business Class upgrade?

    To request an upgrade, simply fill out the online form and upload your flight ticket as a PDF or screenshot.

    If you're unable to upload your ticket, check the box “Enter ticket details manually” — this will allow you to provide your:

    • Full name
    • Booking Reference (PNR)
    • Flight date and route
    • Airline
