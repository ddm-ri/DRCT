Frequently Asked Questions

  • Can I sell airline tickets without IATA accreditation?

    Yes. DRCT operates as an accredited intermediary, which means you access airline inventory through DRCT's network — not through your own IATA credentials. You don't need IATA accreditation to search, book, issue, and service airline tickets. Setup is straightforward and you can start selling from day one.

  • What are the alternatives to traditional GDS for air content?

    DRCT aggregates content from multiple sources — GDS connections, NDC direct API integrations, and low-cost carrier feeds — and delivers it through a single interface. You see all available options in one place, without managing multiple connections yourself. This gives independent agencies access to the same range of content as larger, accredited players.

  • Do I need contracts with individual airlines?

    No. DRCT's supply network includes direct agreements with airlines, which means you don't need to negotiate or maintain your own airline contracts. You access inventory through DRCT's accredited relationships. This eliminates one of the main barriers for independent agencies entering new markets or selling a wider range of carriers.

  • How do I manage booking changes and cancellations?

    All post-booking actions — seat changes, name corrections, cancellations, refunds — are managed inside the DRCT platform. For standard changes, agents can act directly from the interface. For complex situations, DRCT's support team is available 24/7 to handle escalations and airline-specific workflows, including refund processing and voluntary/involuntary changes.

  • Can I sell ancillaries and extras — not just the base fare?

    Yes. DRCT enhances airline offers with available ancillaries — baggage, seat selection, meals, upgrades, and other service items — directly in the booking interface. Ancillaries are included in the offer at search time where available, and can be added during booking or post-sale. This lets independent agents compete on the full value of a booking, not just the base ticket price.
