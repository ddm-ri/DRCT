Frequently Asked Questions

  • What airline content is included in the DRCT feed?

    DRCT aggregates content from NDC-enabled airlines, low-cost carriers, and charter airlines. This includes fares, ancillaries, fare conditions, seat availability, and booking classes — delivered through a single normalized API regardless of the underlying airline distribution model.

  • How does fare normalization work, and why does it matter for OTAs?

    Each airline distributes offers in a different format — different field names, pricing structures, ancillary packaging, and fare rule representations. DRCT maps all of these into a consistent schema before delivering them to your platform. This means your front-end and pricing logic only has to handle one format, reducing development overhead and eliminating per-airline edge cases.

  • How does DRCT handle NDC booking and post-booking servicing?

    DRCT provides end-to-end NDC workflow support — from offer search and pricing to order creation, seat selection, ancillary add-ons, ticketing, and post-booking modifications. All servicing operations (changes, cancellations, refunds) are handled via the same API endpoint your OTA uses for search and booking.

  • How long does it usually take to integrate your API?

    Most OTAs complete their initial integration within a few weeks. The timeline depends on the complexity of your existing systems and how many workflows (search, booking, servicing) you want to integrate at launch. DRCT provides dedicated technical support and documentation throughout the integration process.
