Frequently Asked Questions

  • How do IATA agents access NDC content through DRCT?

    Through DRCT BT or DRCT Extension, depending on your workflow. DRCT BT is a full booking interface with direct access to GDS, NDC, and airline content in one place. DRCT Extension brings NDC content into your existing GDS screen — no new system required. Both tools are connected to the same normalized content layer, so offers are consistent regardless of which tool your agents use.

  • What does NDC integration for agencies actually involve?

    NDC integration through DRCT means connecting to airline offers via modern API — accessing richer content, ancillaries, and bundle structures that aren't available through traditional GDS. DRCT handles the complexity: normalization, offer assembly, and delivery. Your agents see clean, comparable results. You don't need to integrate directly with each airline.

  • How do I manage NDC orders — changes, cancellations, and refunds?

    DRCT centralizes order management in one interface. For NDC bookings, agents can handle standard post-booking actions — seat changes, name corrections, cancellations — directly from the DRCT interface. Complex cases, including airline-specific workflows, are supported by DRCT's operations team available 24/7.

  • Why do NDC bookings sometimes fail, and how does DRCT handle this?

    NDC booking failures can occur due to airline system timeouts, offer expiry, or pricing discrepancies. DRCT monitors booking flows in real time and provides clear error messaging so agents know what happened and what to do next. For critical issues, DRCT's support team can intervene directly — including with the airline — to resolve the problem without requiring agent action.

  • Does DRCT handle the full NDC accounting workflow?

    Yes. DRCT manages NDC BSP reporting alongside GDS transactions in the same system. This means reconciliation is consistent and auditable across all booking types. DRCT also supports back-office integration for agencies using accounting systems that need structured transaction data.
