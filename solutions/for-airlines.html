Frequently Asked Questions

  • Can airlines fully replace GDS with NDC?

    Most airlines cannot fully replace GDS overnight. A gradual GDS to NDC migration is more realistic, allowing airlines to maintain agent coverage while increasing NDC volume. A hybrid approach ensures operational stability and avoids disruptions during replacing GDS with NDC.

  • How does hybrid airline distribution (GDS + NDC) work in practice?

    In hybrid airline distribution, GDS and NDC content coexist in one platform. DRCT enables mixing GDS and NDC content, providing a unified view for agents while simplifying operations. This approach ensures consistent content distribution across all sales channels.

  • How can airlines transition from GDS to NDC without losing agent coverage?

    Airlines can transition using a phased approach with NDC integration layered on top of existing GDS workflows. DRCT ensures agents continue working in familiar environments while airlines scale NDC adoption without losing distribution reach.

  • How does DRCT help reduce airline distribution costs?

    DRCT reduces costs by acting as an airline aggregator and shifting bookings from GDS to NDC and direct channels. This lowers reliance on intermediaries and optimizes ticket distribution, helping airlines decrease per-booking costs as NDC volume grows.

  • How does DRCT support ancillary sales, bundles, and personalized offers?

    DRCT enhances NDC aggregator capabilities by enabling real-time bundles, ancillary upsells, and personalized pricing. We ensure these offers are consistently delivered across all channels, improving conversion and revenue per booking.
