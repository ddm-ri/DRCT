About Emirates

Emirates is the flag carrier of the United Arab Emirates and one of the world's largest long-haul airlines, based at its hub in Dubai. The airline operates a wide-body fleet to more than 140 destinations across six continents, with a strong focus on premium cabins and connecting traffic between Europe, Asia, Africa, and the Americas.

FAQ

  • How to access Emirates via DRCT?

    Contact the DRCT team to set up access. Agencies, OTAs, and TMCs can connect via DRCT BT, DRCT API, or the DRCT Extension depending on their integration type. No separate Emirates contract is required.

  • What Emirates NDC content is available?

    All Emirates fare families — Economy, Business, and First Class — are available through DRCT's NDC connection, including branded fare attributes and ancillaries. Content is consistent across all DRCT products.

  • Are Emirates ancillaries supported?

    Yes. Seat selection, extra baggage, meal preferences, and lounge upgrades are available at booking and post-ticketing through the DRCT platform, with no separate booking flow required.
