About Lufthansa

Lufthansa is the flag carrier of Germany and one of the largest airlines in Europe by fleet size and passengers carried. The airline operates a global network spanning more than 200 destinations across Europe, the Americas, Africa, Asia, and the Middle East. Its hubs in Frankfurt and Munich connect long-haul and short-haul traffic across the Star Alliance network.

FAQ

  • How do I access Lufthansa via DRCT?

    Contact the DRCT team to set up access. Agencies, OTAs, and TMCs can connect via DRCT BT, the DRCT Extension, or the DRCT API depending on their integration type. No separate Lufthansa contract is required.

  • Which Lufthansa Group carriers are included?

    All four carriers — Lufthansa (LH), SWISS (LX), Austrian Airlines (OS), and Brussels Airlines (SN) — are available through a single DRCT integration with no additional setup per carrier.

  • Are Lufthansa ancillaries supported?

    Yes. Seat selection, extra baggage, meal preferences, and other Lufthansa Group ancillaries are available at booking and post-ticketing through the DRCT platform, with no separate booking flow required.
